Product researchMobile captureStructured report

01 / THE IDEA

FROM SCREENS TO A STORY.

A product teardown is easiest to trust when the observations stay connected to the actual journey. This tool keeps screenshots, notes, and timestamps together as I move through an app, then gives that raw record a clear structure.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

CAPTURE. ORGANIZE. SHARE.

The flow is designed for one-handed use on a phone. I capture a screen, write a quick note, and keep moving. The report groups findings while retaining the original observations, so readers can trace a conclusion back to what was seen.

03 / THE BUILD

A TOOL FOR BETTER PRODUCT CONVERSATIONS.

I built the capture flow, report generation, PDF export, and sharing options as one connected workflow. The goal is simple: make it easy to show what happened, what stood out, and why it matters.
